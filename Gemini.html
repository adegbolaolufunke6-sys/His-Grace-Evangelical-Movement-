Welcome to Our World

Get to Know Us

We wish to welcome you to your year of Revitalization. As a church that cares for her members, we are glad to serve you with this information that enables you to know who to call in time of any need.

At His Grace Evangelical Movement, you will find a warm family of believers passionate about worship, the Word, and winning souls. Whether you are visiting for the first time or returning home, there is a place here for you.

But they that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint. Isaiah 40:31 — Our 2026 Anchor
01

Our Purpose

To worship and glorify God, preach, teach and equip the saints to reach the un-reached with the gospel of Jesus Christ and plant self-reproducing churches.

02

Our Vision

Ministering the whole Word to the whole man in the whole world.

03

Our Mission

Win, train, equip and release men and women as world changers to harvest fields. (Acts 17:6b)
